# ADR 0026: Let propagation and aplanatic reference meet in comparison

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-08-12

## Context

Metalens focus evaluation has two different physical inputs: an assembled plane
`Field` propagated through a uniform medium, and an independently authored
aplanatic pupil used to form an ideal reference. Component propagation and
electromagnetic propagation currently cover different applicability and power
semantics. FFT and CZT, in contrast, are distinct numerical realizations of the
same Richards--Wolf method. Segment-level tests do not alone prove that repeated
harness invocations preserve one conduct life from brief to Result.

## Decision

`conduct` remains the sole harness-facing scientific Interface. A harness
repeats it with the byte-identical brief and application root, follows typed
outcomes, and never selects a propagation or aplanatic algorithm. Authority
retains the Study frontier, exact bindings, evidence, and Results; live solver
sessions and numerical caches are not workflow state.

The compiled task and exact binding select field propagation. Component
propagation and electromagnetic propagation remain separate qualified
realizations until explicit low-NA linear, PB-basis, sampling, power, and Result
parity evidence justifies replacement. A parity study may conclude that both
realizations remain necessary; it must not predeclare electromagnetic
propagation as the winner. The parity study records replacement readiness; a
separately accepted successor decision owns any production migration.

One private aplanatic-reference formation Module restores a joint binding. FFT
forms the natural conjugate-grid reference; CZT repeats those exact coordinates
to establish numerical agreement and forms the exact comparison grid. Both
realizations must qualify independently and jointly. They are never averaged,
selected by a caller, or used as hidden fallbacks. The propagated field and
aplanatic reference meet only in an explicit comparison that retains normalized
shape disagreement separately from absolute Poynting power.

The selected complex field and its matching power plane belong to the admitted
focal region. Focus evaluation reads that fact and never propagates again.

The bounded parity decision is `dual_applicability`. On an adequately sampled
transverse-linear field, component and electromagnetic propagation agree to
strict complex-field tolerance. Replacement is nevertheless not natural for
the current 940 nm / 480 nm benchmark sampling or for PB right/left channels
and their converted/retained power meaning. Component propagation therefore
retains the current low-NA and PB roles; electromagnetic propagation retains
its transverse-linear, half-wavelength-sampled, absolute-Poynting-power role.
No route migration or successor proposal follows from this decision.

## Consequences

The internal formation seam may deepen binding validation, shared pupil
preparation, batching, and provenance without becoming a generic solver
factory. FFT, CZT, component propagation, and electromagnetic propagation
remain realization language rather than harness input. Cross-process journey
tests must prove one application root through consultations, evidence pauses,
focus calculation, completion, and terminal replay. A skill owns the concise
typed-outcome cadence; no second harness engine or mutable workflow is added.

This decision refines ADR 0006 without changing its component-based `Field`
language or its `form -> propagate -> evaluate -> conclude` order.

The focal-calculation initiative is not sealed by this parity decision alone.
Real Codex and Claude acceptance and both named Native delivery endpoints are
still incomplete; their absence cannot be represented as a pass.
