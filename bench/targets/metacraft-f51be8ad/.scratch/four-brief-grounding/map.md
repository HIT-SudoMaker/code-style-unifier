# Let four briefs begin from exact material truth

Label: `wayfinder:map`

Status: resolved (2026-08-09)

## Destination

Reach one implementation-ready decision set for four compact, paper-grounded
briefs whose user facts, harness proposals, material bindings, period choices,
height choices, stopping boundary, and later cleanup each have one owner.

## Notes

- Use the canonical language in `CONTEXT.md` and accepted ADRs. The former
  four-brief qualification specification is a stale historical input until a
  fresh implementation handoff supersedes its Yun and order-ceiling clauses.
- Preserve each paper case's reviewed truth outside production. A compact
  blind brief may choose independent design facts; the benchmark alignment
  records every relationship without renaming the brief.
- Distinct material families may explicitly select the same exact native
  record. This is reviewed registration, not aliasing or fuzzy matching.
- Keep Rust frozen and preserve `brief → study → result`.
- Planning only. The two explicitly abandoned sibling directories recorded by
  the cleanup decision are the sole execution exception. Source changes,
  consultation runs, Lumerical solves, sweeps, and field delivery remain
  outside the decision map.
- Use `grilling` and `domain-modeling` for human decisions. Use a bounded
  read-only task for facts available only from the installed Lumerical build.

## Decisions so far

- [Let briefs name science and registrations name products](decisions/01-let-briefs-name-science-and-registrations-name-products.md) — Paper material intent remains unchanged; silica-family intents receive explicit registrations and may share the confirmed Palik record.
- [Let cleanup remove only abandoned work](decisions/02-let-cleanup-remove-only-abandoned-work.md) — Cleanup removes only work whose disposition and retained evidence have been verified.
- [Let the installed library name the remaining materials](decisions/03-let-the-installed-library-name-the-remaining-materials.md) — Installed material inspection fixed the usable silicon and titanium-dioxide records and left a-Si:H honestly unavailable.
- [Let one brief distinguish facts, preferences, and omissions](decisions/04-let-one-brief-distinguish-facts-preferences-and-omissions.md) — Users own goals, the harness may propose one accepted implementation premise, and only period then height remain consultation omissions.
- [Let target-near briefs declare material adaptations](decisions/05-let-target-near-briefs-declare-material-adaptations.md) — Yun and Arbabi use silicon in their blind briefs while retaining a-Si:H as published truth; TiO2 selects the reviewed Siefke record.
- [Let one ordinary McClung brief replace Yun](decisions/06-let-one-ordinary-mcclung-brief-replace-yun.md) — A compact 550 nm SiN brief replaces Yun without adding a reproduction identity or geometry template.
- [Let sampling bound the period and order bound the proof](decisions/07-let-sampling-bound-the-period-and-order-bound-the-proof.md) — Sampling alone bounds legal periods; order regime selects the evidence required for a complete-field claim.
- [Let four briefs stop before periodic evidence](decisions/08-let-four-briefs-stop-before-periodic-evidence.md) — Four replayable material, period, and height decisions close this phase before cell sweeps or fields.
- [Let cleanup follow the four-brief baseline](decisions/09-let-cleanup-follow-the-four-brief-baseline.md) — Explicitly abandoned siblings leave now; reproducible caches and run evidence wait for the replacement baseline.

  invented native names.
- Editing the scientific content of presentation files while cleaning the
  code workspace.
- Rust changes, CST work, live solver sweeps, or four-case delivery.
- Continuous-achromatic production types, GaN or sapphire registrations,
  IRUE or hexagonal-lattice templates, and spectral orchestration. The
  method-level discussion remains a deferred Research Record.

  calculation, and paper-efficiency acceptance in the current phase.
