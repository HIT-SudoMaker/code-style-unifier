# Let briefs name science and registrations name products

Label: `wayfinder:grilling`

Status: resolved (2026-07-30)

Superseded in part by
[Let target-near briefs declare material adaptations](05-let-target-near-briefs-declare-material-adaptations.md)
on 2026-08-08. Published references still name paper materials; the Yun and
Arbabi blind briefs now deliberately use silicon and declare that difference
as an adapted benchmark alignment.

## Question

Should the four paper briefs change their material families to fit the two
registrations already present, or should the project material library
explicitly register the exact Lumerical records selected for the briefs'
unchanged scientific material intent?

## Resolution

The four paper briefs retain their material families and paper identity. The
project material library changes to meet them.

`silica`, `fused silica`, `silicon dioxide`, and `glass` remain distinct
canonical families and may each explicitly select
`SiO2 (Glass) - Palik`. Sharing one exact native record is an intentional
reviewed selection, not an alias or automatic substitution.

The remaining atom families require their own exact native names confirmed
against the installed Lumerical build before registration.
