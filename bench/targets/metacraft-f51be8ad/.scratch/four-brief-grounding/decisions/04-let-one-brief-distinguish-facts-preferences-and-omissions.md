# Let one brief distinguish facts, preferences, and omissions

Label: `wayfinder:grilling`

Status: resolved (2026-08-09)

## Question

What must every canonical metalens brief state as a binding scientific fact,
what may it state as a design preference, what may remain honestly omitted
for Adviser recommendation, and which paper facts must remain outside the
compiler as comparison truth?

## Resolution

One harness intake separates three owners before compilation.

1. The user owns the desired performance and every hard process constraint.
   Aim, objectives, wavelength, numerical aperture, focal length, incident
   polarization requirements, requested quantitative performance, and any
   declared fabrication or budget limit are never filled from a convenient
   default. Missing values return to the user as fields to supply. Exact
   arithmetic may derive one value from user facts only when its inputs and
   relation remain visible.
2. The harness may propose one source-grounded implementation premise:
   control strategy, meta-atom shape, atom and substrate material families,
   material sources, and conservative fabrication values. The user accepts or
   edits that premise once before it becomes brief truth. A web source can
   justify a material recommendation but cannot replace a reviewed material
   registration, optical sample, or material binding.
3. Only cell period and atom height remain scientific consultation omissions
   in the four benchmark briefs. After material binding, period advice
   precedes one deterministic period choice; only that choice permits the
   height domain, height advice, and one deterministic height choice.

The harness constructs one complete canonical `MetalensBrief` only after the
first two owners are resolved. The compiler receives that brief directly; no
`DraftBrief`, form workflow, mutable intake state, or second lifecycle is
introduced. Aperture and solver preference may remain honestly omitted.
Solver preference is an execution preference and does not become scientific
design meaning.

Published period, height, efficiency, geometry, and device-scale facts remain
owned by the external benchmark case. They do not direct consultation or
become acceptance thresholds for the blind brief.

## Consequences

- The harness asks users for missing goals instead of manufacturing them.
- One accepted design premise avoids a sequence of shallow confirmation
  prompts while preserving user authority.
- Material choice is settled before period, and period before height.
- The four-brief stage can test grounded judgment without opening a cell
  sweep or field calculation.
