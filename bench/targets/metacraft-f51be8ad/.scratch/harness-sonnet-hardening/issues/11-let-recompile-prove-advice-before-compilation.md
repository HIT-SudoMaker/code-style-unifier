# Let recompile prove advice before compilation

**Parent specification:** [Harness-native Sonnet hardening](../spec.md)

**Status:** resolved (2026-08-09)

**Assignee:** Codex

**Blocked by:** [Let two advice shells close through one core](09-let-two-advice-shells-close-through-one-core.md), [Let one typed fault cross the answer seam](10-let-one-typed-fault-cross-the-answer-seam.md)

**Implementation authorization:** Explicit owner approval of the parent
specification is required before any code, test, or canonical-document edit.

## What to build

Deepen `MetalensEvidence.recompile` so every retained period and height advice
is authenticated through Authority, re-formed against current domains and all
closed research modes, reconstructed as an exact answer, accepted through the
same question-owned rules, and compared byte-for-byte before the regenerated
value reaches `compile_metalens`.

Keep period-before-height and propagation-only envelope restoration explicit.
Add stable direct period/height request-stale and replay-mismatch reasons. If
the checkpoint codec adds `study_frontier_invalid`, require the direct replay
fault as its cause. Prove replay is read-only and cannot reopen a consultation.

Add the full period/height by recommendation/`EvidenceRequired` behavioral
matrix plus forged Study bytes, missing Authority body, duplicate advice,
research-mode mismatch, stale domain/envelope, typed answer-rule replay fault,
regenerated-byte mismatch, and unchanged-frontier cases.

Update replay clauses in `DESIGN.md`, `SCIENCE.md`, and `DEVELOPMENT.md` in this
same slice.

## Acceptance

- `MetalensEvidence.recompile` is still the only replay Interface.
- The Study subtree never authenticates retained advice; admitted bytes do.
- Exactly one research-mode-derived request identity matches.
- Only regenerated byte-identical advice reaches compilation.
- Replay mutates no Authority state and never yields
  `ConsultationAnswerRejected` or a fresh request.
- Focused replay/checkpoint/conduct tests and `git diff --check` pass.

## Exclusions

Do not add stored requests, default research-mode assumptions, compatibility
repair, replay policy/registry/callback/Adapter, compiler Authority access,
public Interfaces, acceptance changes, `CONTEXT.md`, ADR 0021, map, or index.

## Stop condition

Stop when fresh conduct and replay traverse the same question-owned acceptance
rules and unproved retained advice cannot enter compilation by another road.
