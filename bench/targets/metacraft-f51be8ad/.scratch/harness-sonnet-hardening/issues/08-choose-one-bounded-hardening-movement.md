## Question

How should the resolved decisions become one stable implementation
specification, vertical dependency-ordered tickets, review axes, and final seal
without reopening scientific behavior or leaving two execution roads?

The movement must close every High/Medium contract, resolve or reject every
accepted deepening candidate, preserve frozen Interfaces, define exact tests
and exclusions, keep live harness success non-blocking, and stop when no named
decision or verification gap remains.

## Resolution

Publish [Harness-native Sonnet hardening](../spec.md) as the stable planning
contract and implement it only after explicit owner approval through eight
dependency-ordered tickets numbered 09-16. Do not overwrite the seven resolved
decision tickets or this decision ticket.

The production branch is:

```text
09 private closed-advice core
  -> 10 typed answer fault
    -> 11 Authority-backed current-rule replay
```

The independent acceptance branch is:

```text
12 correction deletion and read-only provenance
  -> 13 two concrete acceptance profiles
    -> 14 recorded nonblocking exact-once campaign
```

The branches join at Ticket 15 for canonical-document reconciliation and the
smallest architectural ratchets. Ticket 16 performs the deterministic final
seal and no semantic repair. This graph is acyclic, has one road at every seam,
and keeps each implementation ticket within one fresh working context.

Freeze the public consultation, conduct, Authority, schema, scientific,
benchmark, solver, evidence, and Result contracts. Keep `CONTEXT.md` and ADR
0021 unchanged. Permit only the accepted private production seam and
acceptance-only seams. The live 2x4 campaign remains a separately authorized,
nonblocking evidence action; its availability or success is not required for
implementation closure.

The movement stops after Ticket 16 records deterministic proof that all named
contracts, deletions, dependencies, documentation, and exclusions hold. It
does not continue into a generic framework, compatibility layer, third
harness, Native execution, or aesthetic cleanup.
