# Evidence-compiled continuous-achromatic metalens

Status: ready-for-agent

## Problem Statement

The user needs MetaCraft to demonstrate that scientific compilation is more
than selecting one familiar phase mechanism. A publication-grade continuous-
achromatic metalens must reconstruct one unrotated full complex Jones response
of the same anisotropic structure from two admitted linear-basis excitations,
preserve the measured and analytical contributions, and compose them into one
fixed device:

- geometry-controlled complex spectral response supplies propagation/resonant
  phase, relative group delay, higher-order residual, conversion, and leakage;
- physical orientation supplies the analytical wavelength-independent PB phase
  under the admitted polarization convention and a validated rotational-
  covariance assumption.

The current continuous route already proves part of this concept, but it is not
ready for a publication freeze. It silently rebuilds a circular grid instead of
consuming the Brief's aperture intent, carries exploratory study and
qualification constants without a traceable specification owner, repeats
eligibility rules, rejects alternative materials by family name before
evidence, and has not completed the Native device and independent-validation
matrix. The current synthetic route also stops at incomplete focus, so it
cannot be presented as a successful continuous-achromatic metalens.

The user also needs the work to remain inside the existing Sonnet architecture.
Adding another harness lifecycle, a third control strategy, a generic optimizer,
or a solver framework would weaken the architectural and scientific claim.
Runtime projections and stale Python caches must not be confused with Authority
truth, especially when an unanswered consultation legitimately contains no
advice.

## Solution

MetaCraft will compile one visible continuous-compensation phase Method through
the existing Brief, Study, conduct, Authority, Evidence, and Result lifecycle.
The Method will use a TiO2-first, evidence-decided spectral campaign. It will
reconstruct the unrotated Jones tensor of each legal anisotropic geometry from
x- and y-polarized observations at design and interleaved-validation
wavelengths, qualify the geometry-controlled spectral response, freeze the
aperture, and only then observe the additional blind dense-verification
wavelengths. It will analytically apply PB orientation without creating
rotation-specific work during candidate formation or aperture assignment.
Publication validation will directly simulate a bounded, predeclared set of
rotated cells without feeding any validation observation back into the design.

One physical Lattice will be resolved after period selection and shared by
monochromatic propagation phase, monochromatic PB phase, and continuous
compensation phase. Each occupied site will retain one geometry and one physical
orientation at every wavelength. Geometry will close the required relative
delay and spectral behavior; orientation will close the reference-wavelength
phase after accounting for the selected geometry's own phase. The Result will
expose the geometry-controlled phase, PB phase, and their realized composition,
along with the fixed aperture, per-wavelength Fields, focal regions, and
complete-band focus evaluation.

The public source-level story will contain three showcase cases: propagation
phase, PB phase, and continuous compensation phase. These showcases will not
replace the publication evidence matrix. Publication readiness additionally
requires a positive Native TiO2 target, same-contract PB-only chromatic
baseline, post-freeze blind verification, correct neighboring refusal,
fabrication tolerance, independent validation, byte-exact replay, repeated
harness conditions, an agent-free baseline, and open release of exact evidence
and failures.

The highest acceptance seam is conduct. A caller supplies one exact Brief and
the existing evidence Adapters, then observes an immutable waiting Study,
ConsultationRequired, a typed evidence or physical stop, or an admitted Result.
Supporting pure Interface tests will cover Lattice resolution, spectral
qualification, response composition, and Result replay without exposing private
implementation placement.

## Implementation Decisions

- Preserve the public Brief-to-Study-to-conduct-to-Result lifecycle. The compiler
  selects the continuous Method from resolved intent; the harness does not
  select an algorithm or own a workflow.
- Keep propagation phase and PB phase as the only public metalens control
  strategies. Continuous compensation phase is a compiled Method that composes
  their relevant physical responses, not a third strategy value.
- Retain the current aim-owned continuous Method Module. Deleting it would
  scatter relationship, preparation, execution, evidence interpretation, and
  conclusion policy back into conduct, so it is deepened rather than split.
- Reuse MaterialResponse and PeriodicResponse. No new solver Adapter is created;
  analytical PB orientation creates no rotation-specific periodic work during
  candidate formation or aperture assignment. Bounded rotated-cell work is
  reserved for post-freeze publication validation and cannot change the design.
- Resolve one physical Lattice after period selection. Focal length, NA, and a
  supplied aperture intent remain exact user facts. Circular half-span is
  derived from focal length and NA; the period determines coordinates; a
  supplied radius or diameter site count must match the compiled central-line
  count.
- Report central span count, storage shape, and occupied-area count as different
  quantities. The legacy Brief's structured 51-site diameter conflicts with
  its 20 micrometre wording and the focal-length/NA/period closure, so it remains
  an exact mismatch case under its existing identity. A new publication Brief
  must explicitly declare or honestly omit the aperture intent. For the 49
  micrometre, NA 0.2, 320 nm-period publication target, the resolved values are
  63 central diameter sites, a 65 by 65 storage shape, and 3069 occupied
  circular sites.
- Make the continuous aperture consume the shared Lattice and an exact Lattice
  reference. It may not derive a private grid from focal length and NA.
- Treat amorphous TiO2 on glass as the preferred first candidate and study seed,
  not as a success predicate. Structural Method applicability is separated from
  material evidence availability and response qualification.
- Permit an explicit alternative material to proceed through the same
  single-rectangle template when its structural and Adapter capabilities are
  available. Otherwise return a typed unsupported outcome or an evidence-backed
  realization refusal; do not infer a universal material judgment.
- Introduce one immutable spectral study specification inside the existing
  achromatic Module. It owns protocol identity, primary-source provenance,
  period policy, the first-slice 600 nm height, fabrication derivation,
  wavelength split, geometry enumeration, response qualification profile,
  work ceilings, and assignment policy.
- For the current seed, enumerate the 136 unequal rectangles formed by legal
  80--240 nm dimensions on the 10 nm grid. The closed maximum is 272 reference
  works, 2176 candidate follow-up works, and 4352 post-freeze verification
  works, or 6800 x/y-polarized periodic works. Reference screening reduces the
  candidate follow-up, and the frozen aperture's used geometry set reduces the
  verification work.
- Use five design wavelengths and four interleaved validation wavelengths to
  form a candidate over the current 470--590 nm band. After the aperture is
  immutable, observe 16 additional blind verification wavelengths so that the
  union is the full 5 nm grid of 25 wavelengths. Blind verification does not
  participate in fitting, eligibility, threshold tuning, assignment ranking,
  or redesign. Sampling roles, execution order, and work ceilings are content-
  addressed planning facts, not mutations of the operating spectrum.
- Preserve the existing serialized holdout wavelength name where compatibility
  requires it, but define those four samples as interleaved validation because
  they gate candidate eligibility. Only the 16 observations first requested
  after aperture freeze are publication-blind evidence.
- Give one versioned response qualification profile ownership of reference
  screening, conversion, leakage, angular-frequency phase linearity,
  interleaved-validation residual, reference-phase coverage, relative-delay
  span, and post-freeze dense-grid residual and curvature rules. Every threshold
  must carry reviewed provenance and freeze before Native results are observed;
  the current exploratory constants do not automatically become publication
  gates.
- Make SpectralLibraryQualification retain the profile reference, per-geometry
  assessments, eligible geometry set, coverage summaries, evidence-completeness
  state, and one candidate or typed-refusal verdict. Candidate authorizes
  aperture assignment; it does not establish publication success.
- Make aperture assignment consume only the qualification verdict and eligible
  set. It does not know or recompute qualification thresholds.
- Separate unit-cell eligibility floors from device-level publication metrics.
  A low converted-power floor may keep a cell available for scientific
  diagnosis, but it cannot establish lens transmission, focus efficiency,
  focal-shift, spot-width, or leakage success.
- Preserve one geometry and one physical orientation at every occupied site
  across every design, validation, and post-freeze verification wavelength.
  Store immutable geometry, orientation, geometry-controlled phase, PB phase,
  target phase, realized phase, required delay, selected delay, and delay-error
  maps.
- Derive the orientation from the admitted polarization convention after
  geometry selection. At the reference wavelength, the realized phase must
  reconstruct exactly from the selected geometry phase and PB phase.
- Retain adjacency diagnostics for the frozen aperture, including dimension
  jumps and transition classes between occupied neighbors. They are validation
  evidence, not inputs to a new optimizer or a license to redesign the layout.
- Form converted and retained Fields from the measured complex Jones response.
  Every spectral Field cites the same fixed aperture and exact response
  observations used to establish it.
- Retain one immutable post-freeze band-verification Evidence record inside the
  existing achromatic Module. It cites the frozen aperture, profile, used
  geometry set, blind observations, complete Field/focus matrix, and pass or
  typed-stop outcome. The used geometry set is the union of every assigned
  geometry and the PB-only baseline geometry. The record creates no second
  conduct lifecycle.
- Retain the compensated and PB-only strategies inside the continuous spectral
  evaluation. Both use the same wavelengths, Lattice, propagation realization,
  normalization, focal region, and focus evaluation.
- Describe the local geometry/orientation mapping as deterministic discrete
  inverse assignment. Do not claim a generic, learned, continuous, topology, or
  self-evolving inverse-design framework and do not introduce a generic
  optimizer.
- Provide three source-level showcases without changing the four frozen
  historical benchmark cases: representative propagation phase, representative
  PB phase, and the continuous TiO2-first route.
- Require eight publication evidence rows: dense-grid Native target plus the
  same-contract PB-only baseline, post-freeze blind verification, neighboring
  refusal, fabrication tolerance, rotated-cell and independent validation,
  resume/replay, and repeated harness plus agent-free comparison.
- Consolidate achromatic Result restoration behind one private implementation
  used by conclusion and replay. The public conclusion and replay Interfaces do
  not change, and replay performs no numerical work.
- Deepen the existing run projection only enough to expose exact references for
  the compiled path. A projection cannot restore or advance a Study.
- Preserve provider-free advice semantics. Empty advice means no validated
  consultation answer is retained. Accepted advice remains untrusted Study
  input and never becomes Evidence.
- Keep the retired provider-owned advice package absent from tracked source.
  Runtime bytecode caches, run manifests, and outcome projections are excluded
  from truth and release inputs.
- A positive publication claim requires complete Native target closure,
  comparable baseline, post-freeze blind verification, independent validation,
  and byte-exact replay. A complete physical refusal is a valid software outcome
  but blocks a successful-device manuscript.

## Testing Decisions

- Test observable scientific behavior rather than private function placement,
  helper count, or mirrored constants. Tests should survive internal
  refactoring while the Module Interface and domain behavior remain unchanged.
- Use conduct as the highest end-to-end seam. It must demonstrate compilation,
  exact task formation, evidence admission, waiting, typed refusal, completion,
  resume, and Result conclusion through the existing Adapters.
- Test Lattice resolution through its shared Interface for matching circular
  radius and diameter intents, matching square intent, honest omission, and
  declared-versus-compiled mismatch. Preserve the legacy 51-site Brief as the
  mismatch case. Confirm that a new publication Brief explicitly declaring or
  deriving the physical aperture resolves to 63 central sites, a 65 by 65
  storage shape, and 3069 occupied sites.
- Test the spectral study specification and plan through canonical round-trip,
  mutation rejection, provenance, exact geometry enumeration, exact work
  counts for candidate and post-freeze phases of the 25-wavelength grid,
  immutable wavelength roles and execution order, missing material evidence,
  empty fabrication domain, and budget refusal.
- Test material behavior through continuous Method outcomes: TiO2 is preferred
  but evidence-decided; an explicit alternative can reach evidence gathering,
  unsupported capability, candidate, or physical refusal without a name-only
  verdict.
- Test qualification through complete candidate, incomplete evidence,
  numerical incompletion, conversion refusal, linearity,
  interleaved-validation refusal, phase-coverage refusal, and delay-span
  refusal. Separately test post-freeze band verification through missing blind
  evidence, dense-grid residual or curvature stop, and pass. Tests freeze
  profile provenance before outcomes and keep cell eligibility distinct from
  device publication success. Assignment tests consume eligible geometry
  without restating numeric gates.
- Test response composition by changing geometry response and orientation
  independently. Verify the admitted PB sign, exact reference-phase
  reconstruction, immutable geometry/orientation maps, and nonzero geometry
  diversity where a candidate exists. Test adjacency diagnostics through the
  aperture Interface without introducing a spatial optimizer.
- Test spectral Fields as a complete strategy-by-wavelength matrix over all 25
  design, validation, and post-freeze blind-verification wavelengths. Every
  entry must cite one frozen aperture, and a missing wavelength in any role must
  stop completion rather than alter an average.
- Test the compensated and PB-only focus summaries under identical propagation,
  focal-region, and normalization contracts. A focus search must span the
  declared axial bracket rather than inspect only the nominal plane.
- Test Result conclusion and replay through their existing public Interfaces.
  Their restored bytes and failures must agree, and replay must not call a
  material Adapter, periodic Adapter, propagator, or focus evaluator.
- Test compiled-path projection as a read-only view. Corrupting or deleting it
  cannot affect resume or replay. Empty advice remains ConsultationRequired and
  cannot advance a Study.
- Test the three showcase cases separately from the frozen benchmark catalogue.
  Existing propagation, PB, benchmark identity, and Result-byte regressions must
  remain green.
- Test the Native publication campaign with exact manifests for design and
  interleaved-validation and post-freeze blind-verification work, failures,
  rotated-cell spot checks, perturbations, comparison contracts, solver
  versions, and checksums.
  Synthetic fixtures establish deterministic behavior but cannot satisfy the
  publication gate.
- Use the current achromatic science tests, conduct tests, Result replay tests,
  Sonnet dependency and ratchet tests, resumable application journey, retained
  harness evidence, and four-case benchmark acceptance as prior art.
- Run focused tests after each ticket. Before the implementation freeze, run the
  complete Python suite with the mandated project interpreter, Pyright,
  architecture and source ratchets, canonical-document checks, Markdown-link
  checks, and whitespace verification.

## Out of Scope

- A dynamic Method registry, planner agent, generic optimizer, provider client,
  second conduct lifecycle, new application root, or new solver Adapter.
- A third public metalens control strategy for continuous compensation.
- Wavelength-dependent geometry or orientation, independently redesigned
  monochromatic apertures, PB phase presented as a source of group delay, or an
  orientation-resolved production search. Bounded post-freeze rotated-cell
  validation is explicitly in scope.
- Paper-exact compound TiO2 fins, polarization-insensitive paired structures,
  GaN integrated-resonant structures, SiN multilayers, freeform topology, or a
  general material-template framework.
- Changing the frozen identities or comparison contracts of the four historical
  benchmark cases.
- Treating advice, a feasibility forecast, a unit-cell candidate, a synthetic
  fixture, a run projection, or a solver receipt as a completed metalens Result.
- Fabrication execution, experimental measurement, journal selection,
  manuscript drafting, or submission before the publication evidence gate.
- Bypassing publisher access controls or redistributing artifacts without a
  compatible license.

## Further Notes

- The current continuous proof tail and accepted architecture decision remain
  the starting point. This specification deepens their evidence ownership and
  publication closure; it does not replace the architecture.
- The first campaign is a local square-period, single-rectangle adaptation of
  reviewed visible continuous-compensation physics. It must not be called a
  paper-exact reproduction unless its structure and evidence genuinely match.
- Continuous-band means that one fixed physical layout is evaluated over the
  dense, predeclared 5 nm grid and passes its interpolation and curvature
  checks. It is not presented as a mathematical proof at every real-valued
  wavelength.
- The current exploratory synthetic library reaches spectral field formation
  but stops at incomplete focus. That is an honest evidence boundary, not an
  implementation success.
- For presentation, the propagation phase map, PB phase map, and continuous
  realized phase should be visibly separate. For science, the continuous
  response is established from one full unrotated Jones tensor reconstructed
  from two basis excitations plus analytical orientation, not from measuring or
  combining two unrelated post-processed devices.
- Empty advice in an unanswered run is expected. The durable source is the
  current Study and admitted Authority records; local runtime caches are
  disposable and excluded from implementation and archival decisions.
- Once implementation and deterministic tests close, run the Native campaign.
  Manuscript work begins only after positive target closure, fair baselines,
  post-freeze blind verification, independent validation, and replayable open
  artifacts.
