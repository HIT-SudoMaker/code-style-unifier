## Work

Freeze P6--P8: one independent device-scale or cross-method validation of the
fixed aperture, byte-exact resume/replay, repeated harness conditions, and an
agent-free deterministic baseline. Then assemble the lawful open-source/code
and data release.

Prefer a validation that changes the numerical model rather than merely
rerunning the same local-periodic-to-ASM pipeline. Record the transfer contract,
mesh/convergence checks, permitted approximations, comparison metrics, and every
disagreement. If available resources cannot support a whole-device Maxwell
check, state the narrower independent method and limit the manuscript claim.

## Acceptance

- The frozen aperture is validated without redesigning it.
- Nominal/independent differences are reported under one predeclared metric and
  tolerance contract.
- Replay fetches exact Authority records and performs no material solve,
  periodic solve, propagation, or focus search.
- Repeated harness and agent-free runs use comparable Briefs, evidence, budgets,
  stop rules, and deterministic evaluators.
- Release manifests include schemas, raw receipts, stops/failures, fields,
  focus data, scripts, environments, checksums, licenses, and reproduction
  instructions without credentials or machine-local paths.
- Manuscript drafting is explicitly blocked unless P1, P2, P3, and P6 are
  positive and replayable.

## Non-goals

No fabrication claim without fabrication, no hidden cleanup of failed runs, and
no unsupported statement that the independent method is full Maxwell.
