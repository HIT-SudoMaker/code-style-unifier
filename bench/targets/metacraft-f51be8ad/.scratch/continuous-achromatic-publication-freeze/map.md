# Freeze the evidence-compiled continuous-achromatic metalens

Label: `wayfinder:map`

Status: ready-for-agent

## Destination

Reach one implementation-ready publication-freeze specification for a visible,
transmissive continuous-achromatic metalens that compiles exact user intent into
one physical aperture and a TiO2-first, evidence-decided spectral proof.

The specification must state the Native, independent-validation, comparison,
refusal, replay, and open-release evidence required before manuscript writing
may begin.

## Notes

- This map plans the next closure. It does not implement code, run Native work,
  fabricate a device, or draft the manuscript.
- Preserve the public `brief -> study -> result` lifecycle, the Sonnet conduct
  seam, the harness-owned consultation model, and the existing material and
  periodic-response Adapters.
- Preserve the physical invariant: every aperture site has one geometry and one
  orientation shared by every design and holdout wavelength.
- Treat titanium dioxide as the preferred visible-light candidate, not as a
  name-based success fact. Material and cell performance remain evidence-decided.
- Remove silent research-policy constants, not fixed physical layout or closed,
  versioned Method contracts. Do not introduce a dynamic registry, AI planner,
  solver factory, or second lifecycle.
- Use `CONTEXT.md`, ADRs 0004, 0014, 0018, 0024, and 0028, the previous
  [continuous-achromatic map](../continuous-achromatic-metalens-wayfinding/map.md),
  the [competitor and material Research Record](../../docs/research/2026-08-15-wayfinder-agentic-metasurface-and-achromatic-materials.md),
  and the `grilling`, `domain-modeling`, `codebase-design`, `research`, and
  `nature-academic-search` skills.
- The working paper thesis is evidence-governed scientific compilation, measured
  by physical success, false completion, correct refusal, replay, recovery, and
  evidence completeness rather than agent count or prose quality.

## Route

1. Deepen the existing aperture Module so every metalens Method consumes one
   resolved physical Lattice.
2. Freeze one content-addressed spectral study specification and one
   qualification verdict.
3. Expose the compiled scientific path without making a projection
   authoritative.
4. Run the Native publication matrix, independent validation, replay, and open
   release.

The implementation-ready draft is the [publication-freeze
specification](spec.md).

## Not yet specified

- The journal tier, fabrication commitment, and final manuscript claim depend on
  the Native candidate/refusal outcome and the selected independent validation.
- The exact blinded agent-evaluation sample size and statistical design depend on
  whether this work claims inverse design or evidence-governed design.
- The final paper figure set and manuscript outline depend on the observable
  compiled-path projection and completed proof matrix.

## Out of scope

- Holographic metasurfaces, quasi-BIC metasurfaces, absorbers, frequency-selective
  surfaces, multifocal lenses, and tunable lenses.
- A complete GaN, SiN, or Si3N4 implementation; these remain independent material
  precedents unless a later map explicitly admits one.
- CST, COMSOL, RCWA, another solver Adapter, a dynamic Method registry, or a
  second conduct lifecycle.
- Exact reproduction of Chen, Wang--Tsai, MetaChat, Self-Evolving, or MetaDesigner.
- Manuscript drafting, journal submission, and fabrication execution.
