### Phase one — correct, deepen, qualify

Tickets 01-05 repair the current facts before adding orchestration evidence.
They move from comparison, through retained propagation and coordinates, into
one aplanatic-reference formation Module and joint realization binding.

Tickets 06-08 prove cross-process conduct continuity, sharpen the repository
Skill around the observed cadence, and make the propagation parity decision.

rewrite or a conditional propagation migration.
