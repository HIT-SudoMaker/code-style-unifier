# 06 — Let one surface retain what G0 cannot

**What to build:** Carry a qualified sampled complex reference-surface
response from the solver boundary into aperture-field formation when one G0
coefficient cannot establish the declared complete field.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Let four cases state one honest truth.

**Status:** resolved (2026-07-30)

- [ ] ADR 0009 remains unchanged: the current G0-only complete-field method
      hard-rejects a period outside its compiled zeroth-order limit.
- [ ] The richer response is a new route-neutral physical capability and does
      not rename propagation or geometric phase.
- [ ] Reference-surface evidence retains sampled complex field, exact surface,
      coordinate frame, medium, component basis, requested input basis,
      sampling, and source references.
- [ ] Existing periodic transmission and periodic polarization capabilities
      remain independent and retain their own qualification fixtures.
- [ ] The richer capability is issued only after its own native fixture
      succeeds and cannot be inferred from a successful G0 fixture.
- [ ] Malformed, incomplete, non-finite, foreign-basis, or mismatched-surface
      observations are rejected before authority admission.
- [ ] Aperture field formation consumes the admitted response form without
      reducing a sampled patch back to one complex coefficient.
- [ ] Multi-order regime and locally periodic assembly limitations remain
      visible through study, evidence, result, and replay.
- [ ] A bounded small-aperture full-wave fixture reports complex-field and
      power differences against locally periodic patch assembly.
- [ ] The bounded comparison is diagnostic evidence, not whole-device FDTD
      and not a hidden success threshold.
- [ ] Adapter absence uses the existing typed outcomes; no common solver error
      hierarchy, compatibility alias, or strategy registry is added.
- [ ] Focused response, evidence, aperture-field, and replay tests pass with a
      fake engine; live Lumerical remains disabled.
