# Let evidence close before live work opens

Label: `wayfinder:grilling`

Status: resolved (2026-07-30)

## Question

What evidence closure and delivery gate keep material reuse fast without
letting stale or approximate material facts reach a canonical case?

## Resolution

A selected SolverMaterial is admitted as an immutable record. The
LumericalMaterialSample cites that registration and one exact solver binding;
its identity includes both registration and wavelength. MaterialBinding cites
the sample and solver binding. Replay follows admitted references without
opening the project library, Adviser, Lumerical, or Torch.

Malformed catalogues and invalid read-back fail directly. Missing
registration, missing native material, and uncovered wavelength remain
distinct waiting reasons. Ticket 10 material smoke may open after this
architecture is verified and one complete atom/substrate pair is registered;
each canonical case opens its sweep only when its own complete pair is
confirmed. Missing materials for a sibling case do not block the ready case.
