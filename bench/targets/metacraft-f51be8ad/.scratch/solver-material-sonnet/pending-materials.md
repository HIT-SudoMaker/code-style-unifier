# Unregistered published material family

The published Yun and Arbabi references retain this exact material family:

- hydrogenated amorphous silicon

The active blind briefs intentionally adapt it to `silicon`. Local Lumerical
25v2 inspection found no exact default a-Si:H record, so this family remains
unregistered until a reviewed custom or imported dataset is selected. It must
not be aliased automatically to crystalline silicon.
