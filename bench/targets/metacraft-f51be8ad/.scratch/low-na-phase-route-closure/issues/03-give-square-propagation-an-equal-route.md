# 03 — Give square propagation an equal route

**Type:** implementation

**Status:** wontfix

**Superseded by:** [Four-brief delivery ticket 05](../../four-brief-metalens-delivery/issues/05-let-circle-and-square-share-one-propagation-proof.md).

**Blocked by:** tickets 01 and 02.

## Outcome

A square propagation pillar is an equal cell geometry, not a special example.
It passes through the same compiler, evidence, quantized matching, Field, and
Result route as a circular pillar.

The Pi 2025 square-pillar example is the tracer bullet.

## What to build

- Use the existing science `Cell` as the admitted fabrication identity in the
  propagation response library, aperture, fabrication output, and Result.
  Pre-evidence plans and product constructions stay internal.
- Preserve a typed square-pillar identity from brief compilation through
  candidate generation, Lumerical construction, evidence, selected library,
  aperture assignment, fabrication output, and Result provenance.
- Bind the example's amorphous-silicon atom and silica substrate through the
  requested-material qualification from ticket 02.
- Reuse the propagation-phase policy: choose several cells for each requested
  quantization and treat phase cyclically at `0 == 2π`.
- Keep the public route geometry-neutral where behavior is shared; place only
  genuinely shape-specific construction behind the Adapter seam.

## TDD seam

Begin with the Pi brief at `conduct`. If tickets 01 and 02 already make this
test pass, do not invent square-specific implementation: pin the regression,
verify exact replay, and close the ticket. Ticket 07 owns the public example
catalogue. Otherwise fix only the first seam that rejects, renames,
duplicates, or flattens the geometry.

## Acceptance

- The Pi example completes independent 8-, 12-, and 16-state fake conducts.
- The selected library and aperture assignment retain square width and height
  through one stable admitted Cell identity.
- Cyclic phase distance is shared with the circular propagation route.
- Circular and square examples use the same public compilation and conduct
  interfaces.
- No paper-efficiency value is treated as an acceptance threshold.
- Focused tests, architecture tests, Pyright, and CSU on touched files pass.

## Do not add

- A square-specific workflow or compiler.
- A second public full-cell type that duplicates the route-neutral Cell.
- A generic geometry registry.
- Live Lumerical execution.
- Paper reproduction claims.
