# Find the continuous-achromatic metalens compilation road

Label: `wayfinder:map`

Status: ready-for-agent

## Destination

Reach one implementation-ready, publication-oriented specification for a
continuous-achromatic metalens that preserves the four monochromatic proofs,
lets one metalens aim compile compositional user intent into an auditable
scientific proof, and defines the first locally adapted periodic design and
its independent validation evidence.

## Notes

- Experimental implementation and Native probes live only on
  `experiment/continuous-achromatic-metalens`; integration and paper claims
  wait for the evidence frontier to close.
- Use `CONTEXT.md`, ADR 0004, ADR 0010, ADR 0024, the repository research
  records, and the `grilling`, `domain-modeling`, `research`, and
  `codebase-design` skills.
- Preserve one public `brief -> study -> result` lifecycle. Generic dispatch
  stops at `aim`; metalens science owns its Brief language and proof choice.
- Treat the Wang--Tsai work as primary scientific input that may be adapted to
  qualified local materials and an explicitly chosen lattice. Do not silently
  call an adaptation a reproduction.
- Keep holography, BIC, absorbers, FSS, CST, COMSOL, new solver Adapters,
  multifocal metalenses, and tunable metalenses outside this destination.

## Decisions so far

- [Let one metalens aim compile compositional user intent](decisions/01-let-one-metalens-aim-compile-compositional-user-intent.md) — One aim-owned compositional Brief separates user facts from Design, proof, bounded planning, response evidence, and Result while generic dispatch remains aim-only.
- [Define the orthogonal lattice promise](issues/02-define-the-orthogonal-lattice-promise.md) — The first Method fixes one square lattice through template applicability and planning rather than exposing an unsupported lattice choice in the Brief.
- [Research square-template PB compensation](issues/06-research-square-template-pb-compensation.md) — Reuse the existing square realization and analytic PB road, but require planned full-band Jones evidence and bounded physical-rotation qualification before continuous-band assignment.
- [Draw the paper-to-local material adaptation line](issues/03-draw-the-paper-to-local-material-adaptation-line.md) — Preserve the PB-plus-dispersion invariants while binding local materials and describing changed devices as adaptations rather than reproductions.
- [Make method resolution dual and auditable](issues/04-make-method-resolution-dual-and-auditable.md) — One spectrum-valued Brief compiles through Design-first typed method assessment, with compact forward-mutation and reverse-reference proof and no public rule engine.
- [Keep spectral work out of the Brief](issues/05-keep-spectral-work-out-of-the-brief.md) — Brief owns the claimed band; Method owns spectral physics; one CellStudyPlan owns bounded samples and work; evidence, one physical layout, wavelength Fields and the band Result remain separate facts.

## Resolution and remaining frontier

The first local feasibility slice in issue 07 is resolved by the experimental
branch and its retained Native material/reference-screen evidence. Issue 08 is
the remaining publication-proof frontier; the branch is not merge-ready.

- [Choose the first local feasibility slice](issues/07-choose-the-first-local-feasibility-slice.md) — Fix the modest local band, focal geometry, reviewed material family, fabrication constraints and user acceptance targets without copying a paper device.
- [Freeze the publication proof ladder](issues/08-freeze-the-publication-proof-ladder.md) — Fix the positive case, neighboring typed refusal, baselines, holdout and independent validation needed before specification and tickets.

## Out of scope

- Holographic metasurfaces, quasi-BIC metasurfaces, absorbers, and frequency
  selective surfaces.
- CST, COMSOL, RCWA, or another new product Adapter.
- Multifocal, varifocal, or actively tunable metalenses.
- Exact reproduction of a published material stack or geometry as a mandatory
  product requirement.
- A public rule engine, dynamic method registry, AI planner, solver factory,
  or second conduct lifecycle.
