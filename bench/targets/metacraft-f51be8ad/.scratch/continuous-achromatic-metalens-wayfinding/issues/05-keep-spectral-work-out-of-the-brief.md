# Keep spectral work out of the Brief

Status: resolved (2026-08-14)

Assignee: Codex

Label: `wayfinder:grilling`

Blocked by: [Define the orthogonal lattice promise](02-define-the-orthogonal-lattice-promise.md), [Make method resolution dual and auditable](04-make-method-resolution-dual-and-auditable.md)

Parent: [Find the continuous-achromatic metalens compilation road](../map.md)

## Question

After the Brief declares a continuous operating spectrum, which exact spectral
facts belong to resolved Design, compiled Study, bounded CellStudyPlan,
PeriodicResponse evidence, one physical Aperture, wavelength-specific Fields,
and the terminal continuous-band Result?

## Resolution

The Brief owns only the continuous claim interval, fixed-focus geometry,
illumination and output requirements, material and fabrication constraints,
optional mechanism constraints, budget, honest omissions and any explicit
user acceptance criteria.  It owns no lattice kind, reference wavelength,
spectral grid, holdout points, cell candidates, fitting policy, solver work or
algorithm selection.

`MetalensDesign` normalizes those exact user facts and derives only
aim-resolved physical quantities such as a consistent aperture/NA/focal
geometry.  It does not copy capabilities from an already selected
Relationship.  The compiled method assessment and Proof own the phase law,
phase convention, reference frequency, legal spectral gauge, necessary delay
span, applicability conditions, qualification profile and typed refusal
meaning.

One admitted `CellStudyPlan` owns the exact square period and height, primitive
rectangle candidates, material binding, response channels, design and holdout
wavelength samples, convergence schedule, work count and projected
single-wavelength `PeriodicWork` identities.  The existing `PeriodicResponse`
remains one wavelength and one geometry's raw solver-neutral observation.
Qualified spectral Jones evidence owns ordering, complete provenance, phase
branch, converted response, slope, higher-order residual, power, leakage,
holdout and physical-rotation verdicts.

One response-free physical aperture layout owns exactly one geometry and one
orientation at every site and is immutable across the band.  Wavelength views
combine that layout with one exact spectral-response slice to form the
existing single-wavelength `Field`, `FocalRegion` and `Focus` facts.  The
continuous-band conclusion owns the exact wavelength-to-reference mapping,
worst wavelength, focal drift, spot, converted power, leakage, all omissions
and the terminal pass or evidence-backed refusal.  It never averages away a
failed wavelength.
