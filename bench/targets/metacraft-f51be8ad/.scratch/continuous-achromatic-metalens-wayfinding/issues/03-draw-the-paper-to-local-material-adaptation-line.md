## Question

Which Wang--Tsai scientific invariants may define the new Method, which
published geometry and material choices may seed locally qualified candidates,
and what differences require the result to be described as an adaptation
rather than a reproduction?

## Resolution

Borrow the physical invariants rather than the published device identity.  The
first local Method preserves one fixed passive layout across the band, a PB
orientation term that supplies the reference-frequency phase intercept, a
geometry-dependent complex Jones response that supplies spectral slope and
higher-order residual, one legal aperture-wide spectral gauge, and independent
full-band focal validation.  Conversion, leakage, power, phase-branch,
rotation and local-periodicity qualifications remain required evidence.

Published square-cell dimensions, dielectric heights, rectangle ranges and
material stacks may seed a bounded local consultation or candidate plan.  The
actual Method must bind MetaCraft's reviewed local material records and one
qualified square-period realization; it may change band, period, height,
material family and primitive dimensions when those changes are explicit and
requalified.  No paper value becomes a hidden Brief default or a substitute
for observed evidence.

The result is an adaptation whenever the local material stack, operating band,
geometry library, period, height, simulation convention, assignment or device
validation differs from the publication.  A paper name may label an external
benchmark alignment, but it is not the Brief, Method, Design, Route or Result
identity.  MetaCraft may claim reproduction only after an explicit comparison
contract establishes the published configuration and compatible measures.
