## Question

What exact positive closure, neighboring evidence-backed refusal, PB-only and
chromatic baselines, design/holdout split, local-periodicity transfer checks,
full-device validation and reproducibility gates are sufficient to graduate
the achromatic road into an implementation specification and publication
claim?

## Current evidence boundary

The experiment branch has fixed user intent, Method/Plan ownership, full-band
materials, Jones power normalization, reference-screen-first compilation,
design-only phase fitting, holdout residuals, and candidate/refusal thresholds.
It has not closed the publication ladder. The next implementation slice must:

1. expose per-cell numerical time/convergence failure as a typed periodic
   outcome so a screen can retain completed cells without exception-text
   classification or blind retry;
2. complete one bounded non-reference spectral follow-up for the eligible
   single rectangles and admit either a candidate or typed physics refusal;
3. only after a candidate, implement one immutable layout across wavelengths,
   per-wavelength Fields/foci, PB-only and chromatic baselines, and independent
   transfer/full-device validation.

## Resolution

The accepted proof ladder is frozen in the parent [continuous compensation
specification](../spec.md). The implementation movement is tracked by
[Close the continuous compensation aperture and focus](09-close-continuous-compensation-aperture-and-focus.md).
