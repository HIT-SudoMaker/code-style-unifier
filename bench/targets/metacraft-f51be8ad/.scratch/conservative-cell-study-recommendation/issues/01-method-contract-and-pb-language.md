# Establish the route contract and PB language

Status: resolved (2026-08-10)
Labels: `ready-for-agent`
Depends on: none

Unify the public route vocabulary around propagation phase and PB phase. Keep
the current air-side circular metalens Method invariant and record that
non-air or non-circular designs require a new Method decision. Add a
versioned qualification-profile value that can be supplied by a user-owned
objective or reviewed Method contract; do not invent universal efficiency
numbers. Preserve serialized identities through explicit compatibility or
rejection. Add domain/architecture tests for the contract and naming.
