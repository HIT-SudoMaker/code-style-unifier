# Let physics bound a conservative starting study

Label: `wayfinder:research`

Status: resolved (2026-08-09)

Assignee: `/root/research_conservative_cell_recommendation`

Map: [Let one conservative cell study begin every metalens design](../map.md)

## Question

Which primary-source-supported quantities may MetaCraft use as hard legality,
soft recommendation forecasts, and final solver evidence when proposing a
bounded starting cell study for propagation-phase and PB-phase metalenses, and
which apparently convenient heuristics must remain non-authoritative?

## Resolution

The supporting Research Record is
[Conservative metalens cell-study recommendation](../../../docs/research/2026-08-09-conservative-metalens-cell-study-recommendation.md).

MetaCraft may refuse exact user, sampling, fabrication, grid, orientation
clearance, and declared study-extent violations. Propagation effective-index
span and PB effective-birefringence burdens are route-specific forecasts
that may rank but never qualify a cell. Only complex periodic transmission or
two-basis Jones response closes the corresponding response claim.

The initial lateral study must name exact legal geometries and exact response
tasks before execution. Deterministic coverage may reduce the complete legal
domain, but no primary physical source establishes a universal 16-, 32-, or
64-task limit. That limit must come from a user declaration or a separately
benchmarked execution extent and remain visible as operational policy.
