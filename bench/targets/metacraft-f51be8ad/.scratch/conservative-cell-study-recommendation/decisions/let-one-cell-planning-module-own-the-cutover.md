## Resolution

Introduce one deep, pure `cell_study` Module after an admitted
`PeriodChoice`. Its external Interface is limited to forming a decision card
and accepting an exact answer. It owns height/fabrication derivation,
route-specific forecasts, bounded candidate selection, response needs, and
work arithmetic. `periodic_request` becomes a projection Adapter; solver,
Authority, filesystem, run manifests, network research, and harnesses remain
outside the Module. Old period/height roots are either explicitly migrated or
rejected by identity; they are never silently reinterpreted. ADR 0011,
CONTEXT, tests, and serialized schemas must change together in the
implementation cutover.

## Question

Which canonical values, Interface operations, dependency direction, error
semantics, ADR refinements, glossary changes, four-case acceptance checks, and
old period/height roads belong in the final replacement specification so that
the migration is deep enough to restore coherence but small enough to remain
reliable?
