## Resolution

Propagation response assessment searches a deterministic global phase offset
for each of 8, 12, and 16 levels, then applies cyclic half-step tolerance,
distinct-cell identity, and useful-power/leakage criteria. The 3pi/2 span for
eight levels is a diagnostic necessary-condition heuristic only. PB assessment
requires a complete x/y Jones pair, total transmission, converted power,
retained leakage, and the adopted retardance/cross-coupling profile; it
filters qualified cells before deterministic ranking. No qualified PB cell is
an honest typed finding, not a least-bad choice. Failed response sets may
motivate a cited, user-accepted expansion plan, never an automatic expansion.

## Question

After one bounded periodic study, how should propagation phase assess distinct
8-, 12-, and 16-level coverage under half-step cyclic tolerance and an
explicit global phase offset; how should PB phase assess Jones conversion and
leakage instead; and which failed outcomes permit a cited bounded expansion,
require a user decision, or remain exploratory artifacts that cannot close a
Result?
