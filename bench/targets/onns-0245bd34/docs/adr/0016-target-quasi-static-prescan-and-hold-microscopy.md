# Target quasi-static prescan-and-hold microscopy

The temporal LC-SLM research line targets stationary or slowly drifting microscopy specimens rather than atmospheric or other high-bandwidth aberrations. For each locally isoplanatic field and depth, independently read phase-diverse calibration frames will select a deployable phase-only correction; that mask is then held fixed while one or more separate science frames are acquired. The exposure ceiling remains `T≤8`, and calibration time, dose, camera reads, SLM settling and mask reuse are part of the comparison budget.

This scope accepts LC-SLM latency in exchange for high spatial degrees of
freedom and low-cost science-path integration. It rejects real-time,
Shack–Hartmann-equivalent and globally reusable correction claims:
instrument-, slide- and local specimen-induced aberrations are separate
calibration levels. The biological modality remains an open experimental
decision within this accepted quasi-static boundary.
