# Test calibration-to-science causal transfer

## Question

Does a correction selected from reference-on calibration frames improve a
separately acquired, later raw reference-on science frame under randomized
safe, sham, opposite, and equal-RMS controls, while a separately budgeted
reference-off ablation confirms the interferometric mechanism?

## Kill rule

If calibration evidence does not transfer prospectively to the independent
science observation, redesign the topology or stop the claimed method.
